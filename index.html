Vex

Vex is a moody yordle mage in League of Legends who wields shadow magic to punish mobile enemies, spreading chaos with her apathetic, gloomy attitude.

Lore: Vex is a shadow-wielding yordle who despises joy, spreading gloom and fear wherever she goes.

Yasuo

a wind-sword-wielding wanderer in League of Legends, falsely accused of murder and driven by guilt, seeking redemption while mastering the art of wind technique..

katarina

Akali

Akali is a nimble assassin in League of Legends who uses stealth, speed, and precision to eliminate her targets.

Lore: Akali is a stealthy Ionian assassin who fights to protect her homeland while following her own path.

fizz

Fizz is a playful amphibious trickster in League of Legends who uses agility and aquatic magic to outsmart foes..

Skills ⚡

Charm (Ahri)

Ahri blows a kiss that damages and charms enemies.

Noxious Trap (Teemo)

Teemo places explosive mushrooms that poison enemies.

Final Spark (Lux)

Lux fires a massive beam of light dealing heavy damage.

Judgment (Garen)

Garen spins rapidly, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

Mistral Shadow (Vex)

Vex launches her shadow to damage and fear enemies.

Steel Tempest (Yasuo)

Yasuo thrusts forward with a whirlwind attack that can knock up enemies.

Shunpo (Katarina)

Katarina teleports to a target and deals damage instantly.

Five Point Strike (Akali)

Akali throws kunai in a spread, dealing damage to enemies.

Playful / Trickster (Fizz)

Fizz leaps onto a target, dodging attacks and dealing damage.
